Tile Merge

Slide all tiles in one direction. Two equal tiles that touch merge into one with double the value. Reach the goal tile before the board fills up.

Free play

Swipe, press the arrow keys or use the buttons to slide every tile. Equal tiles that collide merge. After each move a new 2 or 4 appears. Reach the goal tile (128, 256 or 512) before no move is left. Faster finishes score more.

How to play+
  1. Slide the tiles with a swipe, the arrow keys or the buttons.
  2. Merge equal numbers to build bigger ones.
  3. Reach the goal tile before the board is full.
Tips and tricks+

A few ideas that make Tile Merge easier and more fun:

  • Keep your biggest tile in a corner and build next to it.
  • Choose two directions you use most and rarely slide against them.
  • A crowded board can often be saved. Look for merges before you worry.
About Tile Merge+

Play Tile Merge online

Tile-merging puzzles became hugely popular on phones and in browsers. Biuno keeps the simple idea, with gentler goals and no countdown.

Why it works+

You plan several slides ahead and keep the board tidy. The task uses planning, spatial reasoning and weighing short-term gains against room for later.

These are the skills used during play; improvement outside the game is not established.
