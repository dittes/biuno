Golf Solitaire

Play a card from the bottom of a column onto the discard pile if it is one rank higher or lower. Draw from the stock when you are stuck, and try to clear every column.

Seven columns of cards lie face up. Only the bottom card of each column can be played. Tap it when its rank is exactly one higher or one lower than the card on the discard pile; it moves there and becomes the new top. Suits do not matter. On Easy an ace and a king count as neighbours. If you cannot play, draw a card from the stock. Clear every column to win; the more stock cards you have left, the higher your score.

How to play+
  1. Tap a bottom card that is one rank higher or lower than the pile.
  2. Draw from the stock when no card fits.
  3. Clear all the columns. Use Undo if you take a wrong turn.
Tips and tricks+

A few ideas that make Golf Solitaire easier and more fun:

  • Look ahead before you play a card. Taking a card can uncover the one you need next.
  • Keep the draw pile for when you really are stuck, since every stock card left adds points.
  • Plan runs: a sequence like 5, 6, 7, 6 can clear several columns in a row.
About Golf Solitaire+

Play Golf Solitaire online

Golf is a patience game where the cards are dealt face up and you aim for the lowest score, as in the sport. Biuno only deals hands that can be cleared with the right moves.

Why it works+

You decide the order in which to clear the cards. The task uses planning and attention to sequences.

These are the skills used during play; improvement outside the game is not established.
