Bridges

Connect the numbered islands with straight bridges. Each island must carry exactly its number of bridges, bridges may not cross, and every island must be reachable.

Tap an island, then tap another island in a straight line from it to build a bridge. Tap again for a double bridge, and once more to remove it. Each island must end up with exactly as many bridges as its number. Bridges run only horizontally or vertically, never cross another bridge or island, and at most two may join the same pair. When every island is satisfied and all are connected, you win.

How to play+
  1. Tap an island, then another in a straight line.
  2. Tap again for two bridges, once more to remove them.
  3. Meet every number and link all the islands.
Tips and tricks+

A few ideas that make Bridges easier and more fun:

  • An island with 1 in a corner can only reach one neighbour. Start with those.
  • If an island has as many bridge slots as its number, put a bridge in every direction.
  • Check connectivity at the end. Two satisfied groups that are not linked mean something has to change.
About Bridges+

Play Bridges online

Bridges is known as Hashiwokakero in Japan, meaning build bridges. It appeared in Japanese puzzle magazines in the 1990s. Every board here can be solved.

Why it works+

You count what each island still needs and trace connections. The task uses logic and spatial reasoning.

These are the skills used during play; improvement outside the game is not established.
