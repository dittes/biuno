Colour Flood

Start in the top-left corner. Each move recolours your growing area. Flood the entire board before your moves run out.

Your area starts in the top-left corner. Choose a colour: the whole area takes it and absorbs every neighbouring square of that colour. Make the board one colour within the move limit. Easy is 8×8 with 5 colours, Standard 10×10 and Hard 12×12 with 6. Every colour also has its own shape.

How to play+
  1. Choose a colour below the board.
  2. Your area turns that colour and absorbs matching neighbours.
  3. Cover the whole board before the moves run out.
Tips and tricks+

A few ideas that make Colour Flood easier and more fun:

  • Prefer colours that absorb many squares now and open up more later.
  • Aim for big groups of one colour. A single move can swallow them.
  • Do not ignore single squares: they can be bridges to large groups.
About Colour Flood+

Play Colour Flood online

Flooding puzzles are casual-game favourites: one corner grows by absorbing neighbours of the colour you pick. Each colour also has a shape, so colour is never the only clue.

Why it works+

You look ahead to see which colour opens up the most squares later. The task uses planning, pattern recognition and weighing options.

These are the skills used during play; improvement outside the game is not established.
