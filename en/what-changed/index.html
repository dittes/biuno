How to play+
  1. Look carefully at every picture. Take as long as you need.
  2. Press Find the new picture. One picture changes and the cards move.
  3. Select the picture you did not see before. Complete five challenges.
Tips and tricks+

A few ideas that make What Changed? easier and more fun:

  • Scan the pictures in the same order every time, for example left to right, then top to bottom.
  • Build tiny stories: “a rocket above a doughnut”. Stories stick better than single pictures.
  • There is no timer, so take the study phase slowly. A relaxed look beats a rushed one.
About What Changed?+

Play What Changed? online

This belongs to the “what changed?” family of observation games played with pictures or objects on a table. Here one picture is replaced, and moving the cards makes position alone an unreliable clue.

Why it works+

You compare a remembered set of pictures with a new set. The task uses visual attention, recognition and short-term retention rather than speed alone.

These are the skills used during play; improvement outside the game is not established.
