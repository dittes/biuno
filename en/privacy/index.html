Privacy

Your play stays yours.

Biuno does not ask for an account. The game code uses localStorage in your browser to save scores, settings, daily results and unfinished rounds. These records are not sent to a Biuno game server. No advertising, analytics scripts, external fonts or tracking cookies are added by Biuno.

A service worker caches the site and game files on this device for offline use after a successful first visit. Browser settings may remove or block saved data. Private browsing may erase it when you close the session.

The hosting provider may process connection data, such as your IP address and request time, to deliver and secure the site. A private preview may additionally require the hosting provider’s sign-in. Browser-local progress does not sync between devices.

For questions about this site or personal data, contact the operator listed in the imprint.
